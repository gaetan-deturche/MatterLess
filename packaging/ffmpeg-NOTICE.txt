MatterLess plays videos with FFmpeg, https://ffmpeg.org

  Version:  n9.0.2-3-ga5923073bf
  Build:    decoders only, by tools/build-ffmpeg.sh in the MatterLess
            repository, with dav1d 1.5.1 (https://code.videolan.org/videolan/dav1d)
            linked into avcodec
  Files:    avcodec-63.dll, avformat-63.dll, avutil-61.dll, swresample-7.dll,
            swscale-10.dll

FFmpeg is free software licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1 or later. Its text is in ffmpeg-LICENSE.txt, beside this file.
dav1d is licensed under the BSD 2-clause licence, in dav1d-LICENSE.txt.

The DLLs are loaded at run time: you may replace them with your own build of
FFmpeg of the same major versions.

The corresponding source code is attached to every MatterLess release as
ffmpeg-source-a5923073bf.tar.gz, and is also available from
  https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/a5923073bf
with the script it was built with at
  https://github.com/gaetan-deturche/MatterLess/blob/main/tools/build-ffmpeg.sh
